# Hotel Kamala & Resto — Official Website

Official website for **Hotel Kamala & Resto**, located on NH-48 near Apex University, Achrol, Jaipur, Rajasthan.

## Features
- **100% Pure Vegetarian Restaurant Menu**: 115 dishes across 15 organized categories (Specials, Thali & Meals, Soups, Main Course, Breads, Starters, Noodles, Rice & Biryani, Pizza, Pasta, Snacks, Tea & Coffee, Juices & Shakes, Salad & Raita).
- **Interactive WhatsApp Ordering & Estimator**: Real-time cart calculation with automated pre-filled WhatsApp order messages and itemized temporary estimate bills.
- **Authentic Hotel & Room Photos Gallery**: 29 verified photographs of deluxe AC/Non-AC rooms, bathrooms, banquet hall, dining restaurant, and scenic Aravalli hills, complete with touch-friendly lightbox viewer.
- **Rooms Dialog with Video Tour**: Direct video tour of rooms with pricing and direct "View Rooms & Suites Photos" navigation.
- **Responsive Dark/Light Mode Theme**: Seamless theme toggle with localStorage persistence.
- **One-Click Calling**: Direct contact and room booking with Shankar Ji (+91 73573 50916).

## Deployment
This website is completely self-contained and static. You can deploy it to:
- **Vercel** (`vercel deploy`)
- **Netlify** (drag-and-drop or Git repository)
- **GitHub Pages** (Enable in Settings -> Pages -> Source: Deploy from branch `main` / root)
- **Cloudflare Pages**
- Any static hosting server (Apache/Nginx/Caddy)
