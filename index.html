About Us

Hotel Kamala

Welcome to Hotel Kamala, a comfortable and welcoming stay nestled in the beautiful Aravali Hills of Achrol, Rajasthan. Designed to provide a relaxing and convenient experience for travelers, families, and guests looking for a peaceful getaway, our hotel combines comfort, hospitality, and easy access to the surrounding region.

At Hotel Kamala, we offer a range of AC and Non-AC rooms to suit different guest preferences and budgets. Our rooms are designed to provide a comfortable space to relax after a day of travel or exploring the scenic surroundings.

Our in-house restaurant serves delicious meals in a comfortable setting, making it convenient for guests to enjoy their food without having to travel far. The hotel is also equipped with a lift, providing convenient access to different floors for guests.

Why Choose Hotel Kamala?

  • Comfortable AC & Non-AC rooms
  • In-house restaurant
  • Convenient lift facility
  • Peaceful location in the Aravali Hills
  • Located in Achrol, Rajasthan
  • Suitable for families, couples, business travelers, and tourists
  • Warm and welcoming hospitality

Whether you are visiting Achrol, traveling through Rajasthan, or simply looking for a peaceful place to stay away from the busy city environment, Hotel Kamala welcomes you to experience comfort, convenience, and the natural beauty of the Aravali Hills.
